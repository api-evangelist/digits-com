## AI Accounting Glossary

- [AI Accounting Glossary](https://digits.com/glossary/): Authoritative definitions of accounting, AI, and AI-native accounting concepts, including Digits-specific architecture and emerging industry terminology.

- [Agentic General Ledger™ (AGL®)](glossary/agentic-general-ledger-agl): Digits' accounting-specific AI model architecture powering AI-native accounting workflows.

- [AI-Native Accounting](glossary/ai-native-accounting): Accounting software designed from the ground up around AI rather than AI features added later.

- [Agentic Accounting Software](glossary/agentic-accounting-software): Accounting software that uses AI agents to move accounting workflows forward while keeping accountants in control.

- [Agentic AI](glossary/agentic-ai): AI that plans, acts, and adapts within workflows to achieve a goal.

- [Continuous Close](glossary/continuous-close): An accounting approach that keeps books categorized, reconciled, verified, and close-ready throughout the month.

- [Real-Time Financial Reporting](glossary/real-time-financial-reporting): Up-to-date financial reporting powered by continuously maintained books.

- [Truth Drift](glossary/truth-drift): The divergence that occurs when AI outputs become disconnected from the system of record.

- [Trust Layer](glossary/the-trust-layer): The licensed accounting professional who defines quality, verifies outputs, and takes accountability for AI-assisted accounting work.

- [Tiered Intelligence](glossary/tiered-intelligence): Digits' framework for applying multiple layers of accounting intelligence to financial decisions.

- [Ledger-native](glossary/ledger-native): Software or intelligence that operates directly within the general ledger instead of as a separate AI layer, helping keep financial records accurate, current, and continuously ready for reporting.

- [Model Layer Cake](glossary/model-layer-cake): Digits' multi-layer AI architecture that consults specialized models in sequence, from company-specific knowledge to global accounting intelligence.
